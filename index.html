Welcome to the True Crime Archive, an independent digital research project documenting criminal investigations, cold cases, missing persons, and historical mysteries through structured research, timelines, and web-based tools.

The project combines Violet's interests in biology, cultural anthropology, forensic science, and investigative history with frontend development and digital research.
