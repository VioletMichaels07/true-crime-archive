TRUE CRIME ARCHIVE

Independent Research • Cold Cases • Missing Persons • Historical Files

Document. Analyze. Preserve.

Welcome to the archive. This project is an independent effort to document criminal investigations, cold cases, and missing persons cases through organized research and clean web presentation. Maintained out of a dorm room at Duke by an undergrad who spends way too much time reading case files.

Active Research Areas

Cold Cases

Deep dives into unsolved investigations, looking closely at overlooked timelines and evidence.

Missing Persons

Highlighting unresolved disappearances to keep names and details visible in the public eye.

Historical Files

Examining major criminal investigations and mysteries from decades past.

Forensics & Data

Breaking down the science—from genetic genealogy to modern ballistics—used by investigators.
