About the Project

Behind the Vault

Welcome. The True Crime Vault is an independent research project and digital archive built as a creative outlet alongside my studies.

What started as a personal fascination with cold cases, investigative history, and forensic psychology evolved into a structured digital archive. My goal is to present thorough, respectful, and deeply researched breakdowns of historical and contemporary mysteries, keeping a focus on the facts and the human element behind every file.
