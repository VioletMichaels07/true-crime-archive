About Violet Donna Michaels

The Researcher

Violet Donna Michaels is a Duke University undergraduate and the creator and maintainer of the True Crime Archive.

Her academic interests include biology and cultural anthropology, with additional interests in forensic science, investigative history, criminal investigations, and the ways technology can be used to organize and understand information.

The True Crime Archive brings those interests together in an independent digital project focused on documenting publicly available information about historical cases, cold cases, missing persons, and investigative methods.

About the Project

What began as an interest in cold cases and investigative history developed into a structured digital archive. The project combines research, organization, writing, and frontend development to make case information easier to explore.

The archive is an independent student project and is not affiliated with a law-enforcement agency, government organization, or investigative authority.

Research Interests

  • Biology and forensic science
  • Cultural anthropology
  • Cold cases and missing persons
  • Investigative history
  • Forensic technologies and methods
  • Digital research and information organization
